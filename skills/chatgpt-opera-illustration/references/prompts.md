# Prompt recipes

## Reusable style block

> Create ONE original finished editorial illustration. The attached images are STYLE references, not compositions to copy. Match their refined contemporary Chinese theatre-poster language: large confident interlocking color shapes, graceful tapering curves, elegant long silhouettes, expressive simple profiles, small coral cheeks, broad ivory water sleeves and restrained ornament. Deep ultramarine, muted slate blue, coral, pale blush, mustard gold, soft lilac and warm ivory; choose a few dominant colors. Matte flat designed forms with selective fine dry pigment grain at a few edges and folds. Most areas remain calm. One strong visual idea, intentional negative space, clear at thumbnail size. No plastic 3D, glossy highlights, photorealistic face shading, doll eyes, fantasy jewelry overload, disconnected limbs, generic clipart, fake writing, logos or watermark. Output [ASPECT RATIO], one full composition, not a contact sheet. [SUBJECT + ACTION + SPATIAL RELATIONSHIP].

Choose a subject recipe; do not combine every recipe into one prompt.

## Two performers

> A young woman on the left turns toward a scholar on the right; they exchange a quiet glance across a small area of negative space. One relaxed lifted arm leads into a broad ivory water sleeve attached to her cuff, curving behind the pair. The scholar answers with a restrained hand gesture. Natural shoulder/elbow/wrist connections, delicate coherent hands, feet connected to their own hems on one ground. Small profile faces and a few headdress accents. Compose the figures large. Only two or three ornament lines on the robes. Background color fields respond to the sleeve. Interpretive opera imagery, not a reconstruction of a named performance.

## Lake / landscape

> A Jiangnan lake described by broad winding slate-blue, ivory and lilac water shapes. A low band of ultramarine hills, a small cluster of pale waterside houses far away, one cropped mustard field in the sky. One coral five-petal plum rests on the foreground water with a restrained curved ripple. No boats, crowd or large photorealistic architecture. Build depth through scale and overlap, not atmospheric blur or realistic reflections. The lake shares the cloth's calm graphic rhythm.

## Instrument

> One [INSTRUMENT], its construction and proportions guided by the supplied factual reference. Keep strings, bridge, resonator and bow/fingerboard relationships coherent where applicable. A large readable silhouette against two broad ivory/lilac cloth shapes and one muted gold field. Sparse marks describe material; no plastic shine, ornamental extra strings or invented hardware. No lettering. The factual reference controls the object; the illustrated reference controls visual treatment.

## Story / still life

> An open book becomes a small abstract theatre: a few coral curtain shapes and a deep ultramarine stage emerge from the pages. One ivory sweep connects page and curtain without becoming a separate object collection. The book remains coherent, with an obvious spine and connected pages. Generous calm color fields. If a plum motif is requested, place one clear five-petal blossom on a page with space for a later camera move.

## Targeted correction

> Edit the supplied illustration. Change only [FAULTY REGION]: [SPECIFIC CORRECTION]. Preserve the composition, facial character, palette, sleeve sweep, background, print texture and all unrelated objects. The correction should share the same flat editorial mark-making. Do not make the whole image more realistic or more ornate.

## 中文调用示例

“用这个 skill 画一张竖版戏曲插图：一位青衣转身回望，水袖从左下绕到右上。靛蓝和暖白为主，少量珊瑚红。侧脸简洁，衣料有形但不要写实，不要字。”

“沿用这组图的风格，画一个没有人物的江南湖景，留一朵清楚的梅花作为后续运镜锚点。不要照搬上一张的构图。”
